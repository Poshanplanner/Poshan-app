Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

This policy covers one-to-one consultations and Poshan Pro subscriptions.

One-to-one consultations

  • Before the call is scheduled: cancel within 24 hours of payment by WhatsApp or email for a full refund.
  • After the call time is fixed: you can reschedule once, free, by telling us at least 6 hours before the call.
  • If we cannot schedule your call within 3 days of payment, or the doctor decides a teleconsultation is not suitable for you, you get a full refund.
  • Missed calls: if you do not join or answer at the agreed time and do not reschedule, the fee is not refunded.
  • After the call has taken place: the fee is not refundable, because the service has been provided.

Poshan Pro subscriptions

  • Cancel any time from your account. Renewal stops, and Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid for.
  • Payments already made for the current period are not refunded, except as below.
  • If you are charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged in error, write to us within 7 days for a full refund of that charge.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
